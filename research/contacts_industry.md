# Orbital Curbside: industry, agency and insurer contacts

Compiled 16 Oct 2026 for customer-validation interview requests (DECA Innovation Plan).

How this was checked:
- Every channel below was read on a page fetched on 16 Oct 2026, either the organisation's own website or a press release the organisation published itself. The source URL is given in each row.
- No address was guessed or built from a name pattern. Where a page only offers a form, the row says "contact form only".
- Named people appear only where the organisation itself prints their work email as a press or business contact. Otherwise a general, press or partnerships inbox is used.
- "Not found" means no channel could be verified on the organisation's own site. Ask a teacher or mentor for an introduction instead.

## Notes

- **Count:** 36 organisations checked. 32 have a verified channel (25 by email, 7 by contact form only). 4 have none: Swarm/SpaceX, US Space Force, Gallagher and Assure Space.
- **Start with insurers and EP suppliers** for cost questions. Beazley (it already insures on-orbit servicing), Marsh (`NewSpace@marsh.com`), Busek and Enpulsion are the most direct. Ask a short, specific question such as "a rough price range for a 1 kW Hall thruster and PPU" or "would removing your client's dead satellites change their in-orbit premium?"
- **Most likely to reply to a student:** non-profits and agencies with education or general inboxes (Secure World Foundation, ESA `education@esa.int`, Space Data Association) and start-ups' general or press inboxes (Starfish, ClearSpace, Katalyst, KMI's form).
- **Least likely:** SpaceX/Swarm, US Space Force and SDA, and large listed operators (Iridium, Globalstar, ORBCOMM). Their press contacts mostly serve journalists, so keep the email to five sentences, say you are a high-school DECA competitor, and ask for a 15-minute call or 3 written answers.
- **Use press inboxes politely.** Several channels (Iridium, ORBCOMM, Atrium, AXA XL, Global Aerospace) are press contacts. Ask them to forward your request to the right team rather than asking the press officer technical questions.
- **Recent changes to mention in your paper:** NASA OTPS closed (Mar 2025); UKSA merged into DSIT (Apr 2026); Iridium is being acquired by Rocket Lab (Jul 2026 release); the Aerojet commercial propulsion business is now "Rocketdyne" (Aug 2026).
- Re-check any address just before sending. Press contacts change often.

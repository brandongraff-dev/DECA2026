How this was checked:
- **VERIFIED** means the name, current affiliation and title were read on an official university, lab or agency page fetched on 16 Oct 2026.
- **PARTLY VERIFIED** means the affiliation comes from a recent official or primary source (a paper, or a competition page run by ESA), but no current official profile page could be fetched.
- **UNVERIFIED** means no current official source was found.
- An email is listed only if it is printed on a page that was actually fetched. Its source is given next to it. No address was guessed or built from a pattern.

## Suggested outreach order (best fit and most likely to reply)

1. **Iskender** (or **Felicetti**) – the closest prior work; ask what is missing between their routing and a price list.
2. **Ceriotti** – the multi-ADR ML sequencing; ask how to sanity-check your GTOC9 benchmark.
3. **Vasile** – the 2017 low-thrust plus J2-drift paper; ask whether the deorbit-kit strategy holds up.
4. **Colombo** – which targets matter (debris index); ask how a customer would rank objects.
5. **Akhil Rao** / **Jia-Richards** / **Abercromby** – these are the most student-friendly, for the economics, propulsion and debris-data angles.
6. **Conte** – a GTOC9 veteran now at a teaching-focused university; he is likely to be open to a student.
7. **McKnight**, **Jah**, **Lewis**, **Linares**, **Izzo**, **Petropoulos**, **Frueh** – high-profile people; keep the email very short and specific.

## Caveats
- Titles change. Recheck each profile the day you send the email.
- Emails taken from papers (Iskender, Borelli, McKnight) are author-supplied and may be out of date. The official profile is the safer first route where one exists.
- For Izzo, the address was read from the ESA page, where it is stored reversed to deter spam. Open the page in a browser to confirm it.
- Petropoulos (JPL) has no public email. JPL staff are usually reached through a mutual contact or at conferences, so a GTOC-community introduction via Izzo may work better.

# 60-Second Backup Recording Plan (if the live demo glitches)

**Record on your Windows laptop** with the built-in **Snipping Tool → Record** (Windows 11) or **Xbox Game Bar** (`Win + Alt + R` starts and stops). Both are free and already installed.
Run the offline demo (`Start Demo.bat`), press `F11` for full screen, and close every notification first.
Save the file as `OrbitalCurbside_demo.mp4` on the desktop **and** a USB stick.

| Time | Screen (what you press) | Voice-over (read this, about 150 words total) |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:12 | Scene **1** (key `1`). Slowly drag to rotate the globe once. | "This is low Earth orbit today: about nineteen thousand objects from the public catalogue. The gold dots are dead satellites and rocket stages. Nobody is coming to collect them." |
| 0:12–0:27 | Scene **2** (key `2`). Let the three blue rings fan apart. | "Earth's bulge twists every orbit's plane, and the speed depends on altitude. These three rings started in the same plane. Twisting a plane with a rocket is brutally expensive. Letting Earth do it is almost free." |
| 0:27–0:50 | Scene **3** (key `3`). Speed **6 days/s**. At about 0:40 click **Follow truck** for 5 seconds, then **Whole Earth**. | "Our orange truck parks in a lane where its plane drifts at a different speed, waits until it lines up with the next rocket stage, hops over, and attaches a deorbit kit. Ten old rocket stages owned by the US, India and France, four hundred ten days, about one hundred thirty-five kilograms of xenon." |
| 0:50–1:00 | Scene **4** (key `4`). Hold on the cost bars. | "Same job, two ways: about ninety-seven million dollars per object with one mission each, about twelve million with one truck, launch included. That's the business." |

**Tips:** rehearse the key presses three times before recording. Record two takes and keep the better one. Check the audio level. If the laptop stutters while recording, switch to the **1 day/s** speed in scene 3 and cut the voice-over to match.

**In the presentation:** if the live demo fails to start within 20 seconds, say "Let me show you the recording" and play the MP4. Don't troubleshoot in front of the judge; setup time counts toward your 15 minutes.

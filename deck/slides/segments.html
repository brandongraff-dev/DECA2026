Orbital Curbside · segment order = who pays first

8

"We have three customer segments, ordered by who pays first. First, space agencies: ESA, JAXA, the UK, NASA, the Space Force. They're already signing removal contracts and buy with milestone payments. Their challenge: many dangerous objects belong to other countries. Second, satellite operators like Iridium and OneWeb, who need a plan for satellites that die early. They're very price-sensitive. Third, insurers, who want to retire risk but will wait for a proven demo. We chose them because they already carry the cost of debris." Q&A backup: Why not start with operators? They have the clearest legal right (they own the objects), but the smallest budgets. Agencies have budgets now. We pursue both, with operators as early design partners.

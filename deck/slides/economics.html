"Now the money. Every number here includes the launch. Today's missions cost forty to ninety-three million per piece. As a sanity check, if our truck picked up just one piece, our model says ninety-seven million: right in line with today. But with ten pickups on one trip, it drops to about twelve million per piece. And if we keep the truck and just send up new kits, about five million, for around fifty pickups. Out of twenty thousand simulated cases, eighty percent land between eleven and fifteen million. The biggest unknown is what the truck itself costs, and that's what I'm working hardest to pin down." Q&A backup: Where the money goes on one route: truck $80M, kits $15M (10 × assumed $1.5M), launch ≈$12.5M ($7,500/kg × 1,650 kg), operations ≈$7M, insurance ≈$7M. Price at a 20% margin: ≈$15.3M. A dedicated rocket instead of a rideshare adds $4–7M per piece.

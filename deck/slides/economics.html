"Now the money. With our truck removing just one object, our model says about ninety-six million dollars, right in line with today's contracts. That's our sanity check. Ten stops on one route: about twelve million per object. Across twenty thousand Monte Carlo runs, eighty percent land between eleven and fifteen million. Reuse the truck for ten routes: about four point four million per object. The biggest unknown is the truck's own cost, fifty to a hundred fifteen million, and that's what I'm working hardest to pin down." Q&A backup: Where does the money go on one route? Mostly truck hardware ($80M base), then kits ($15M for 10 at an assumed $1.5M each), then launch (~$15M). Propellant is under $0.2M. Electric vs chemical changes cost per removal by only ~1–3%; the levers are stops per route and reuse.

"Now the money, and every number here includes getting to orbit. With our truck removing just one object, our model says about ninety-seven million dollars, right in line with today's contracts. That's our sanity check. Ten stops on our first route: about twelve million per object. Across twenty thousand Monte Carlo runs, eighty percent land between eleven and fifteen million. Keep the truck and send it new kits on later rideshares: about five million per object, for about fifty removals before its thrusters wear out. The biggest unknown is the truck's own cost, fifty to a hundred fifteen million, and that's what I'm working hardest to pin down." Q&A backup: Where does the money go on one route? Truck hardware ($80M base), kits ($15M for 10 at an assumed $1.5M each), launch (~$12M at $7,500/kg for 1,650 kg), operations (~$7M), insurance (~$7M). A dedicated rocket instead of a rideshare would add $4–7M per removal. Electric vs chemical changes the price by a few percent; the levers are stops per route and reuse.

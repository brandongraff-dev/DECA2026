"Here's one trip, in four steps. One: the truck rides a shared SpaceX rocket, the cheapest seat to space. Two: it climbs to the junk's height and drifts, letting Earth line up the next target. Three: a robot arm holds the old rocket, and the truck latches on a small rocket kit. Four: the kit fires, the old rocket falls into the atmosphere and burns up, and the truck drives on to the next stop. Ten times per trip." Q&A backup: The kit lowers the stage's lowest point to 250 km so it re-enters within months; heavy objects get a controlled re-entry over the ocean. Kits weigh about 85 kg each on our first route. The arm and capture are the least-proven part; Japan's ADRAS-J2 plans to demonstrate capture around 2027–28.

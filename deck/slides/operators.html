"Operators are our Product A. In 2019, Iridium's CEO said he'd pay to remove his dead satellites, and joked about ten thousand dollars each. But at millions, he said, it doesn't make sense. Twenty-four of those dead Iridium satellites are still up there. On a ten-stop route through them, our model gives about twelve million dollars each, eight times cheaper than one mission per object, but still far above ten thousand. Finding the price operators will say yes to is the first thing my interviews test." Q&A backup: Why would an operator pay at all? To meet the five-year rule, to avoid collision liability and maneuvers near its own fleet, and for reputation. The FCC's 0.9 success rule means some satellites will always fail.

"What could stop us? Four things, and here's what we do about each. Grabbing it: nobody has grabbed a tumbling old rocket yet. Japan plans to try in 2027 or 2028, and we'll partner with whoever proves it rather than invent our own arm. Cost: our prices are estimates, so I'm testing them with experts. Permission: every object belongs to its country, so our first route uses American, Indian and French stages. Falling pieces: heavy objects are steered to burn up over the open ocean. The plan: talk to customers and experts this year, sign a partner, fly a small test trip, then our first ten-pickup truck from 2029." Q&A backup: Why not build the arm ourselves? It's the hardest, least-proven part, and funded companies (Astroscale, ClearSpace) are already on it. Our edge is the route and the pricing. A rideshare as heavy as our truck needs a custom quote above the 831 kg standard port limit.

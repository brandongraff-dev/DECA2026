"So why hasn't anyone cleaned it up? Because it's expensive. Governments have started paying: Europe, Japan, the UK and the US. But each one is a custom mission for one or two objects, at forty to ninety-three million dollars per piece. That's like hiring a whole garbage truck for every trash bag. At those prices, nobody can clean up thousands. Meanwhile the rules just got stricter. In the US, dead satellites must now come down within five years, and up to one in ten satellites is allowed to fail at that. So there will always be some that need a pickup. Analysts put this market at over a billion dollars already." Q&A backup: Contract values: ClearSpace-1 (ESA, 2020) €86M; ADRAS-J2 (JAXA, 2024) ≈¥13.2B; UK ADR (15 Oct 2026) "more than £60m" for two satellites; US SDA disposal service (Starfish, Jan 2026) $52.5M including options. Market reports are paid analyst estimates that disagree, so we don't build on them; we build bottom-up from contract prices.

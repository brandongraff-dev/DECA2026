Ten pickups

Simulated on real 2026 orbits

HOOK (30 seconds, say it before anything else, slowly): "Right now there are about forty-seven thousand tracked objects circling Earth, and a lot of them are dead: old satellites and rocket stages that nobody is coming to collect. Today, removing just one of them is a custom space mission that costs forty to ninety million dollars. So we asked a simple question: what if a spacecraft worked like a garbage truck, running a route and picking up ten pieces on one trip? And what if Earth itself did most of the driving? That's Orbital Curbside." [Pause, then advance.] Delivery tip: make eye contact on "garbage truck". Hold the clicker; do not read the slide.

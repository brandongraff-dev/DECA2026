"This is the truck, from our 3D model. At launch it weighs about one thousand six hundred fifty kilograms, about as heavy as a car. It has electric engines that run on xenon gas and are about five times more fuel-efficient than chemical rockets. A robot arm to hold each old rocket steady. Ten rocket kits, one for each pickup. And solar wings that power the engines. More than half its launch weight is the kits. The fuel for the whole trip is only about two hundred kilograms." Q&A backup: Mass budget (first route): kits 852 kg, dry truck 600 kg, xenon 198 kg. Two Hall thrusters, 0.202 N each class, Isp 1,710 s. "5× more efficient" compares specific impulse (≈1,710 s vs ≈300 s for a chemical engine). An electric truck can run about five routes before its thrusters wear out.

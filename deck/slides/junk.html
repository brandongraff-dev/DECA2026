"This is every object in low orbit from this October's real catalogue. About forty-seven thousand objects are big enough to track. Only about sixteen thousand still work. The rest is junk: dead satellites, used rocket stages and broken pieces. And they all move at about twenty-seven thousand kilometres an hour. Picture a highway where two of every three cars are abandoned wrecks, still moving at full speed." Q&A backup: The speed is basic orbital physics: 7.4 to 7.7 km/s between 400 and 1,000 km. Object counts: ESA Space Environment Statistics (DISCOSweb). Why not let them fall? Above about 800 km, dead objects can stay up for a century or more.

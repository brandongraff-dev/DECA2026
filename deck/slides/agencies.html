III · Customer Segments · Agencies (Product B)

142 abandoned rocket stages share one orbit band. That’s a route.

The SL-8 cluster at 83°, ~960 km: about 204 tonnes of 1.4-tonne Soviet-era upper stages. SL-8 stages appear 11 times on the 2021 “50 most concerning” list. They won’t fall on their own for a century or more.

What agencies need: risk reduced per dollar, proof before scale, milestone payments.

The catch: these stages belong to Russia. Removal needs the owner’s consent, so we start with partner-nation objects and use this cluster as the technical model.

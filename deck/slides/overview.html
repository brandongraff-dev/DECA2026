I · Overview

Space has a trash problem.
We're building the garbage truck.

The problem

Dead satellites and old rocket parts are piling up around Earth, and every crash makes more junk.

Our fix

One spacecraft picks up 10 pieces per trip, like a garbage truck on its route.

Who pays

Space agencies first, then satellite companies and insurers. A fixed fee per piece removed.

The price

$12.2M

per piece in our model, launch included. Today: $40–93M.

Price is our model's estimate (base assumptions, appendix)

"Here's the whole idea on one slide. The problem: dead satellites and old rocket parts are piling up around Earth, and every crash makes more junk. Our fix: one spacecraft that picks up ten pieces per trip, like a garbage truck on a route. Who pays: space agencies first, then satellite companies and insurers, with a fixed fee for each piece we remove. And the price: our model says about twelve million dollars per piece, including the launch. Today it's forty to ninety-three million." Q&A backup: Why "our model"? Nobody has flown this yet. Every number comes from our open-source model, checked against NASA JPL's competition-winning solution, with every assumption in the appendix.

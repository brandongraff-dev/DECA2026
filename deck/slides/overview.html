"Orbital Curbside is a space garbage truck. One electric spacecraft runs a route through a cluster of dead satellites or old rocket stages. At each stop it attaches a small rocket kit that drops the object into the atmosphere to burn up, then it drives on. Customers pay a fixed fee per object, when it's confirmed coming down. We serve space agencies, satellite operators and insurers. And the value is the price: our model says about twelve million dollars per removal, including the launch, versus forty to ninety-three million in today's contracts." Q&A backup: Why "modelled"? Because no one has flown this yet. Every number comes from our open-source model, benchmarked against NASA JPL's competition-winning solution, with every assumption listed in the appendix.

Problem

Dead satellites and rocket stages pile up. Every crash makes thousands more pieces.

Our fix

One spacecraft collects 10 pieces per trip, on a route, like a garbage truck.

Who pays

Space agencies first, then satellite operators and insurers. A fixed fee per piece.

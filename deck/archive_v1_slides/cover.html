DECA Innovation Plan · 2026–27

Low Earth orbit · 2026

Orbital Curbside

The garbage truck for space.

One spacecraft. Ten pickups per trip. Earth does the steering.

HOOK (about 40 seconds; say it before anything else, slowly): "Look up tonight and you won't see it. But right now, about forty-seven thousand objects that we can track are racing around Earth at about twenty-seven thousand kilometres an hour. Two out of every three are junk: dead satellites, old rocket parts and broken pieces. When two of them crash, they shatter into thousands more. And the satellites we use every day, for maps, weather and the internet, have to fly through it. Today, cleaning up just one piece of junk costs forty to ninety million dollars. We're building a cheaper way: a garbage truck for space. That's Orbital Curbside." [Pause, then advance. Look at the judge on "garbage truck for space".]

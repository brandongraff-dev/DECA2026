"Here's what makes us different. Today, every piece of junk gets its own custom mission, at forty to ninety-three million dollars each. We run a route instead. One truck, one launch, ten stops. Because the expensive parts, the spacecraft and the launch, are shared by ten pickups, our model comes out at about twelve million dollars per piece. And we price it like a garbage route: a fixed fee per piece, paid only when it's confirmed coming down." Q&A backup: Is multi-target removal new? The idea has been studied (ESA's GTOC9 contest in 2017, Di Carlo and Vasile, a 2026 IAC paper), and Astroscale and ClearSpace are working on servicing more than one object. We know of no company selling a priced, multi-stop route on today's catalogue; we'd welcome being corrected.
